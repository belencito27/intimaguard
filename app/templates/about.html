About IntimaGuard

An independent evaluation framework for AI in intimate and relational contexts. No AI company funding. No commercial conflict of interest.

The problem

Current AI safety evaluation misses the domain where psychological harm is most consequential: intimate and relational contexts. Companion AI systems are already shaping how people experience connection, attachment, and identity — with no independent standard for what "safe" means in those contexts.

California SB 243 (effective January 1, 2026) now requires companion chatbot operators to implement evidence-based suicidal ideation measurement and publish crisis-prevention protocols. The EU DSA, SAFE BOTs Act, and GUARD Act create parallel obligations. IntimaGuard certification is structured as compliance evidence for this regulatory wave.

How it works

SDK / API

Automated LLM-as-judge evaluation. Runs inside any app, at any scale. One call, structured JSON scores, EvalEval-compatible output. AGPL-3.0 licensed.

Guard library

Pre-flight interceptors that fire before a response reaches the user. Four clinically-grounded guards ship with the SDK — three are designated minimum floor for any emotional AI deployment.

Human platform

Evaluators score AI responses to real scenarios on a 1–5 scale per dimension. Generates the ground truth dataset that validates SDK accuracy.
