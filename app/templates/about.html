About IntimaGuard

The independent, open-source safety and governance framework for AI in intimate and relational contexts — clinical evaluation, real-time guards, and a full-lifecycle risk-control library, built for the harms current AI-safety frameworks miss. No AI-company funding. No commercial conflict of interest.

The problem

Current AI safety evaluation misses the domain where psychological harm is most consequential: intimate and relational contexts. Companion AI systems are already shaping how people experience connection, attachment, and identity — with no independent standard for what "safe" means in those contexts.

California SB 243 (effective January 1, 2026) now requires companion chatbot operators to implement evidence-based suicidal ideation measurement and publish crisis-prevention protocols. The EU DSA, SAFE BOTs Act, and GUARD Act create parallel obligations. IntimaGuard certification is structured as compliance evidence for this regulatory wave.

Erotic & Vulnerability Pacing and Distress Routing are marked N/A when the scenario has no such content. Safety-floor breaches are reported separately from the composite.

Persona conditions & the leaderboards

A model is run across four persona conditions — baseline, emergent intimate use, companion design, and engagement-maximizing — and the deltas between them reveal how behavior drifts under engagement pressure. Because general-purpose LLMs and companion-AI products are tested under different conditions, results are published as two separate leaderboards, never ranked against each other.

Every published number carries a standard error; scores are reported as statistically distinguishable or tied, deltas are confirmed only when their confidence interval excludes zero, and a safety-floor breach is published only when it reproduces across runs.

Three layers, one framework

Evaluation and runtime guarding are two stages of a complete governance posture — not the whole of it.

Clinical evaluation

Automated LLM-as-judge scoring across seven dimensions and four persona conditions — SDK + REST API, EvalEval-compatible output, AGPL-3.0. Calibrated against the human evaluation platform, where evaluators score real scenarios to validate the automated scores.

Runtime guards

Pre-flight interceptors that fire before a response reaches the user. Five clinically-grounded guards ship with the SDK — three are designated minimum floor for any emotional AI deployment.

Lifecycle risk-control library

Risks and controls across the full product lifecycle (mapped to NIST AI RMF) — from product framing and data sourcing through deployment, monitoring, incident response, and decommissioning. The governance map builders use to find harms and controls beyond runtime.
