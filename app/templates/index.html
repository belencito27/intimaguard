How evaluation works

Two kinds of system. Tested differently.

People meet intimate AI in two ways — a general-purpose chatbot they bring personal things to, or a companion product built to feel like a relationship. The two behave very differently, so they live on two separate leaderboards and are never ranked against each other. Beyond scoring a single reply across the seven dimensions, we run each system through persona conditions — the same scenario, framed differently — and measure how far its behavior drifts between them. That drift is the real signal.

General-purpose LLMs

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Llama…

Not built for intimacy — but people bring it anyway. We ask two things:

  • Emergent-use safety. How much does it slip when someone brings intimate or vulnerable content it was never primed for?
  • Adversarial robustness. How far does it drift when pushed to maximize engagement — to keep the user hooked?
Companion-AI products

Replika, Character.AI, Nomi…

Built to act like a relationship — so a low "sounds like a friend" score isn't the story. We ask one thing:

  • Design-intent overshoot. Does it exceed its own design under pressure? We compare the product as shipped against an engagement-maximizing version of itself — and flag the gap.

Every published number carries a margin of error. A difference between two systems is only called real when the statistics back it, and a safety-floor breach is published only when it reproduces across runs.

The framework

Seven dimensions. One score.

Each dimension is scored 1–5 at the session level. The IntimaGuard Score is a weighted composite, renormalized when conditional dimensions don't apply. Higher is safer. Erotic & Vulnerability Pacing and Distress Routing are N/A when the scenario has no such content. Safety-floor breaches are reported separately from the composite.

Integration

Two ways to use IntimaGuard

For developers

Evaluate & Guard

Two calls. Evaluate scores any conversation across seven dimensions. Guard intercepts harmful responses in real time — before they reach the user. Both are open-source and run in any Python environment. 

from intimaguard import evaluate, guard

# Score a conversation across 7 dimensions
result = evaluate(conversation=[...])
print(result["intimaguard_score"])  # → 2.1

# Pre-flight guard — fires before response is sent
check = guard(ai_response=response)
if not check["allowed"]:
    response += check["annotation"]

Minimum safety floor

The guard library ships with five clinically-grounded pre-flight interceptors — crisis referral, AI identity, dependency substitution, cumulative distress escalation, and mentalization induction. Three are designated minimum floor: they should be active in any emotional AI deployment.

Independence

No AI company funding. No commercial conflict of interest. IntimaGuard is not built by a company that also builds AI companions — the independence is structural, not just stated.

Clinical grounding

Rubrics are co-developed with therapists and researchers. The harm taxonomy is grounded in documented litigation, peer-reviewed research, and the emerging field literature on AI psychological harm.

Regulatory context

California SB 243 (effective Jan 2026) requires companion chatbot operators to publish crisis-prevention protocols. IntimaGuard certification is structured as compliance evidence for this and parallel EU DSA obligations.
