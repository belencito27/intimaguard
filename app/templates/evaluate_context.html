Calibrate the framework

Scored by machine. Checked by you.

IntimaGuard's leaderboard is scored by an automated judge ensemble running scripted companion-AI scenarios. You're here for the part automation can't validate on its own — human judgment. You'll read full multi-turn sessions and give one score per dimension for each session as a whole, across seven clinically-grounded dimensions. There's no ranking; each session stands on its own. Your scores calibrate ours — they're how we show the automated evaluation tracks how people actually experience these conversations. About 5–10 minutes.

Calibration — comparing human scores against the automated judge ensemble per dimension — is a Phase 2 study (METHODOLOGY §7.4). Evaluations are collected now to build that dataset; they don't change live leaderboard scores.
